Questions, answered.

How is this different from a normal expense tracker?

Most trackers show what you already spent. Paycheck Survival looks ahead: it counts your bills and savings first, then tells you what you can spend before the next payday.

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No. You enter your own numbers, so there are no bank logins and no account to create.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser’s local database (IndexedDB) on your device. It autosaves and nothing is sent to a server or to Google Sheets.

What if I change phones or clear my browser?

Export a JSON backup from Settings and restore it on any device. You can also export transactions as CSV.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded it runs on your device, and you can add it to your home screen to open it like a native app.

What if my paycheck is late?

Emergency mode helps you prioritize essentials, see which bills can wait and check how long your money will last.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a planning tool based on the numbers you enter. For investing, tax or serious debt decisions, talk to a qualified professional.
